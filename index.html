About
Photographer & Retoucher — NYC

Abeera Khan

a.k.a. “fat snacks”

My name is Abeera, but people like to call me "fat snacks". I'm a 26 year old Photographer and Retoucher based out of New York City.

Currently shooting for State and NEXT, and freelance retouching for BESPOKE, I've also indulged in the fitness industry in both aspects of creative work, and training.

I have been collaborating with professionals in the photo, food, beauty, and fitness industry for some time now and continue to work with agencies, studios, and creatives around the world.

“I like to spend my time traveling, taking pictures, and eating really good food. I'll start writing in third person when I've received a Lucie award.”

If an agency, brand, or a label marketing representative is reading this, I'd like to say I'm pretty great, and yeah, I would hire me.
